In-Scope Assets

Primary Services

  • compliance-svc — regulatory engine, audit trails
  • credit-svc — credit decisioning, bureau data
  • api-gateway — external-facing endpoints
  • dashboard-ui — compliance dashboard (this app)

Data Classifications

  • PII/SPII — SSN, DOB, driver's license
  • Regulatory — audit logs, compliance reports
  • Financial — credit scores, income data
  • Operational — non-sensitive metadata
